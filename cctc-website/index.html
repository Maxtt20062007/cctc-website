05 · FAQ

Questions homeowners ask.

What kinds of jobs do you take?

Interior and exterior painting, deck and fence coating, drywall patching, doors and hardware, small repairs, and materials supply. If you're not sure whether your job fits, send a request and pick "Not sure / other".

How fast will you get back to me?

Every request gets a phone call by the next business day. Requests sent earlier in the day are often called the same day.

How is my price worked out?

After a site survey we write a quote with materials, travel and labour on separate lines. The pricing section above shows the exact formula we use.

What if the job turns out bigger than expected?

We stop and quote the change first. Nothing extra is added to your bill unless you agree to it in writing.

Do I need to buy the paint and materials?

No. We can supply materials at tracked cost and show it on the quote. If you already have materials, tell us in your request.

Which areas do you serve?

Moose Jaw, Saskatchewan. Use the postal code checker at the top of the page. If you're just outside town, send a request anyway and we'll tell you if we can make it work.

Do you do full renovations?

Not yet. Renovations open once we have a standing crew. You can join the waitlist by choosing "Renovation" in the form.
